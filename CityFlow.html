How CityFlow decides

Best route ≠ shortest route

The planner compares distance, traffic and road condition. A slightly longer road can be recommended when it is clearer or in better condition. After comparison, you can open the same trip in Google Maps for live navigation.

Score = Distance × 4 + Traffic penalty + Road-condition penalty
